Dreams on shadPS4 — November 21, 2026

Extract the entire ZIP and open shadPS4QtLauncher.exe. Add your Dreams game
folder, select Dreams and click Play. Keep the extracted folder together.

For 60 Hz rendering, right-click Dreams in the launcher and select
Game-specific Settings > Force 60 Hz Rendering. The option starts off
and applies the next time you launch Dreams.

Press F10 to show or hide the FPS display.

For Quest, connect to SteamVR and put on the headset before or during play.
Take it off to continue on the PC. You can put it back on in the same session.
