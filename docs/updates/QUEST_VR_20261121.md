# Quest VR · November 21, 2026

Dreams can now present both eyes to SteamVR and mirror the game on the PC. The Quest controllers act as Move controllers. The right controller drives the main Imp. Push its stick up for Triangle or down for Circle. Hold the right stick button to recenter the camera and menus. The left X button works for reeling.

This build carries the stereo depth, paint, lighting and skylight corrections, and fixes a stale compute-queue offset that could freeze tracking. The VR launch uses the tested 60 Hz mode. The frame-rate patch respects the game’s permissive VR setting. Desktop play keeps its existing 60 Hz option. Tested with Quest 3, SteamVR and Dreams CUSA04301 v2.64.
